# Lexicon Domain Anchor Expansion Literature Review

## Phase 1 — claims and inertia

1. Common epistemic, cognitive, and discourse questions need distinct routing
   anchors rather than being compressed into claim/evidence or language form.
2. Property, contract, market, labor, and finance require institution-relative
   roles and cannot be reduced to cost/value or policy.
3. Biological organization requires separation among organism, cell, gene,
   development, metabolism, and disease mechanism without importing a taxonomy.
4. Security, artifact production, and education need domain-specific pivots but
   must not become prescriptive guidance.
5. Weather, climate, environment, and Earth system require different temporal,
   spatial, and system boundaries.

Evidence sought: authoritative definitions, established theoretical contrasts,
standards, consensus reports, and contrary scope notes. A skeptic would cite
theory plurality, jurisdictional variation, classification-only frameworks,
and conflicts among disciplinary definitions.

forcing readers to reconstruct missing distinctions and increasing misuse.
