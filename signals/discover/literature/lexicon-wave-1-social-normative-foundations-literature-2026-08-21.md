# Lexicon Wave 1 Social and Normative Foundations Literature Review

## Phase 1 — Claims and inertia

1. Person, group, community, and society require different membership,
   agency, scale, and persistence questions. Confirming evidence: social
   ontology and institutional theory. Skeptical pressure: individualist and
   holistic accounts disagree about what social entities are built from.
2. Norm, convention, custom, practice, and tradition cannot be reduced to
   observed behavioral regularity. Confirming evidence: norm and convention
   theory. Skeptical pressure: fields vary on expectation, sanction,
   acceptance, repetition, and authority.
3. Good, benefit, welfare, harm, and flourishing require an explicit subject
   and evaluative account. Confirming evidence: well-being theory. Skeptical
   pressure: hedonist, desire, objective-list, and anti-welfarist accounts
   disagree.
4. Equality, equity, fairness, justice, and desert are related but not
   interchangeable distributive criteria. Confirming evidence: equality and
   justice theory. Skeptical pressure: competing relational, distributive,
   corrective, sufficientarian, and desert-based accounts.
5. Responsibility, blame, solidarity, reciprocity, conflict, and
   reconciliation require separate agent, relation, control, response, and
   repair questions. Confirming evidence: responsibility, collective-action,
   solidarity, and reconciliation theory. Skeptical pressure: individual and
   collective agency, moral luck, exclusionary solidarity, and peace-versus-
   justice tensions.
