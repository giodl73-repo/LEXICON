# Lexicon Editorial and Domain Expansion Plan Role Check

## Artifact

A bounded canonical-content plan normalizing 37 admitted Tables and proposing
ten source-reviewed domain anchors at candidate maturity.

## Selected roles

| Role | Selection reason |
|---|---|
| Reference Lexicographer | Canonical voice, headwords, senses, and cross-references. |
| Reference Architecture Editor | Multi-entry grammar and scale coherence. |
| Concept & Taxonomy Boundary Editor | Prevents broad domains becoming catalogs. |
| Domain Source Reviewer | High-stakes and specialized source boundaries. |
| Research Integrity & Provenance | Historical parity and exact admission custody. |
| Product Owner | Reader value, batch cost, and scale justification. |

## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 6
P1 blockers: 0 | P2 issues: 14 | P3 notes: 4

Verdict: **APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS**

Top finding: the batch is justified only if each broad domain is narrowed to a
reusable routing contract with authoritative sources and explicit exclusions.

Cross-role consensus: normalize canonical voice without changing historical
evidence, and stop the expansion after the named ten anchors.

## Amendments

1. Use a new immutable admission manifest with exact assurance hashes.
2. Require jurisdiction, account, framework, or source qualifiers where domain
   variation changes meaning.
3. Stop at 101 Tables and return the next gate to external reader testing.
