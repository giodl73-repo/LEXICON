Verdict: **APPROVED**

Top finding: a fixed, fully funded program is compatible with rigorous
candidate-level admission when weak candidates are merged, reframed, or
replaced without pausing unrelated work.

Cross-role consensus: execution should begin immediately; concurrent reader
evidence and qualified domain review improve the work but do not constitute a
new decision gate on whether the roadmap proceeds.

## Amendments applied

1. Preregister both reader rounds and retain null, adverse, abandoned, and
   deviating records.
2. Treat the Table count as a capacity envelope with explicit
   admit/merge/reframe/replace dispositions.
3. Require qualified domain review and comparison frameworks for
   high-consequence boundaries while preserving no-advice language.
