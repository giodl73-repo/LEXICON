# Health, Disease, Disorder, Condition, and Well-Being

Status: candidate anchor entry

## Orientation

Health is a multidimensional state or capacity under a declared biological, functional, experiential, social, or normative account. Disease is a pathological process or state recognized under a medical-scientific account. Disorder is a clinically or classificatorily recognized disturbance under stated criteria. A health condition is a broad status encompassing disease, disorder, injury, pregnancy, aging-related state, or other health-related circumstance under a classification. Well-being is how well a person is doing or experiencing life under an explicit evaluative account.

## Sense table

| Sense | Governing question | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `health` | In which dimensions and under which account is whose health described over what time? | multidimensional health state/capacity |
| `disease` | Which pathological process or state is recognized by which authority and evidence under what criteria? | medically recognized pathology |
| `disorder` | Which disturbance meets which clinical or classification criteria under what system and version? | classified clinical disturbance |
| `health-condition` | Which broad health-related circumstance is in scope under which classification and purpose? | health-domain status umbrella |
| `health-related-well-being` | How well is which person doing or experiencing life in health-related respects under which account? | health-related evaluative state |

## Root factorization

```text
health-state-use
  := person or population, health domain, setting, time, and perspective
   x health account, dimensions, capacities, functioning, experience, and social context
   x pathological process or state, mechanism evidence, course, and manifestations
   x disorder criteria, classifier, threshold, impairment/distress relevance, and exclusions
   x condition type, classification system, code or label, purpose, and scope
   x well-being account, reporter, domains, goals, preferences, and cultural context
   x authority, version, effective time, assessment method, evidence, and uncertainty
   x contested accounts, limitations, provenance, and no-advice boundary
```

## Decisive distinctions

| Pair | Shared feature | Decisive distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Health vs. absence of disease | disease affects health | multidimensional state/capacity vs. one negative criterion |
| Disease vs. disorder | classifications may use both | pathology account vs. criteria-governed disturbance category |
| Disease vs. syndrome | syndromes may indicate disease | pathological process/state vs. co-occurring manifestation pattern |
| Condition vs. diagnosis | diagnoses name conditions | broad classified circumstance vs. clinician's attributed conclusion |
| Health vs. functioning | functioning contributes to health | broader state/account vs. body/activity/participation interaction |
| Well-being vs. clinical status | clinical status may affect well-being | evaluative/person-relative state vs. clinical classification or measurement |

## Dependencies and stopping boundary

Disease Mechanism, Clinical Sign/Symptom/Diagnosis, Functioning/Disability, Welfare, and clinical outcome retain their owners. This Table stops before diagnosing, interpreting symptoms, determining normality, estimating individual prognosis, or recommending prevention or treatment.

## Selection procedure

1. Identify person/population, purpose, health domain, perspective, setting, and time.
2. Select health account, pathology, disorder criteria, broad condition, or well-being question.
3. Record classifier/authority, version, evidence, manifestations, and course separately.
4. Do not infer functioning, disability, quality of life, or need from diagnosis alone.
5. Preserve contested accounts, cultural context, uncertainty, and qualified determination.

## Reference Delta

| Reference form | Typical contribution | Lexicon delta |
|---|---|---|
| ICD or clinical classification | supplies governed diagnostic categories | requires version, purpose, and authority; does not define functioning |
| Pathophysiology | explains mechanisms and disease processes | routes mechanism detail to the biology owner |
| Health/well-being theory | supplies multidimensional and evaluative accounts | keeps clinical and prudential statuses distinct |

## Failure signs

- health means absence of diagnosed disease;
- disease, disorder, condition, syndrome, and diagnosis are synonyms;
- a classification label proves mechanism or prognosis;
- diagnosis automatically determines disability or well-being;
- one historical or cultural normality standard is universalized;
- the Table assesses a person's condition or advises care.
