# Lexicon Funded Domain-Gap Roadmap

Status: internally implemented; fully funded external reader round pending

## Commitment

Lexicon will close the most conspicuous post-sim-69 domain-anchor gaps through
six bounded execution waves. The owner has authorized and fully funded the
program. This decision is fixed: role and source review strengthen execution
but do not cancel, postpone, or repeatedly re-litigate the program.

The program plans 30-36 new candidate-maturity Tables, taking the current 101
Tables to approximately 131-137 before a bounded reconciliation allowance of
up to three additional owners. These numbers are capacity envelopes, not
automatic admission quotas. A weak candidate is merged, reframed, or replaced
by another authorized gap owner; the funded program continues.

The approximately 250-Table first-substantial-volume scale remains a later
portfolio horizon. This program earns the next coherent layer; it does not fill
the remaining count mechanically.

## Reader and product objective

Intended readers are practitioners and generalist researchers trying to select
the right sense of familiar civic, ethical, formal, clinical, linguistic,
cultural, or artistic terms within a few minutes.

The current friction is not merely absent vocabulary. Readers must currently
route these questions through adjacent owners that do not answer the governing
question, or reconstruct distinctions across several Tables. The product
change is a set of recognizable anchor Tables with explicit senses,
factorizations, stopping boundaries, failure signs, and cross-references.

Source review and internal validation can establish coherent ownership,
traceable provenance, contract closure, and usable rehearsal mechanics.
External readers are still required to establish findability, comprehension,
selection confidence, return use, or market value.

## Execution doctrine

1. Start authoring Wave 1 immediately.
2. Run reader gap validation in parallel; it informs wording, ordering, search
   aliases, and replacement choices but is not a precondition for funded work.
3. Admit only concepts with a distinct governing question and defensible
   stopping boundary.
4. Prefer one reusable owner over lists of disciplinary taxa or named members.
5. Keep all new Tables at candidate maturity until source and reader evidence
   support promotion.
6. Preserve Tables and books as the deliverable; software remains validation
   and navigation support.
7. Do not create relations merely because two new Tables share vocabulary.
8. Publish each admitted batch through an immutable manifest, exact assurance
   bindings, the site, and a clean-source PDF.

## Workstream 0 — Parallel reader gap validation

This workstream begins with Wave 1 and never becomes an indefinite authoring
hold. Controlled tasks cover:

- political representation and citizenship;
- fairness and responsibility;
- community, culture, and social norms;
- proof and logical inference;
- treatment, disability, and care;
- syntax, translation, and aesthetic judgment.

Record failed searches, wrong-owner selections, unresolved concepts, reader
language, time to plausible owner, and confidence in sense selection. Preserve
nulls: a proposed gap is not validated when existing Tables route the task
cleanly. Findings may reprioritize or replace a candidate inside the funded
envelope; they do not stop the overall program.

Before each reader round, freeze the task set, participant eligibility,
instructions, measures, success thresholds, exclusions, and analysis plan.
Retain abandoned tasks, nulls, adverse findings, and protocol deviations. The
second round uses a fresh cohort and the frozen post-reconciliation proof.

## Wave 1 — Society, norms, ethics, and justice

Target: 6-7 Tables; projected inventory 107-108.

Candidate owners:

1. Person, Individual, Social Actor, Group, Community, and Society
2. Norm, Convention, Custom, Practice, and Tradition
3. Good, Benefit, Welfare, Harm, and Flourishing
4. Equality, Equity, Fairness, Justice, and Desert
5. Responsibility, Answerability, Blame, Liability, and Excuse
6. Cooperation, Solidarity, Reciprocity, Conflict, and Reconciliation
7. Optional replacement slot: Collective Identity, Belonging, Inclusion,
   Exclusion, and Stigma

Boundary: ethical evaluation remains distinct from legal status, causal
responsibility, organizational accountability, economic utility, and the
existing harm owner.

Reader job: distinguish what kind of social or normative judgment is being
made, who or what bears it, and which authority or evaluative frame governs it.

## Wave 6 — Reconciliation and reader proof

Target: zero to three Tables; projected inventory 131-140.

1. Audit every new sense against all existing owners.
2. Merge or redirect accidental duplicates.
3. Add cross-Table relations only after separate relation review.
4. Update A-Z navigation, search aliases, Reader routes, and cross-references.
5. Run ordinary-language tasks spanning old and new domains.
6. Visually inspect every new Table in the clean-source PDF.
7. Run a second external reader round and record unresolved routing failures.

An additional reconciliation Table requires evidence of a structural hole; a
remaining numerical allowance is not an admission reason.

## Per-Table admission contract

Every proposed owner requires:

- a recognizable reader question and headword family;
- a governing question distinct from existing owners;
- explicit broader, narrower, related, contrasting, and commonly confused
  concepts;
- an inclusion rule and stopping boundary before subtype authoring;
- authoritative sources plus comparison or contrary frameworks where the
  field varies;
- qualified domain review for legal, political, clinical, disability,
  religious, cultural, and other high-consequence boundaries;
- the complete Lexicon Table grammar;
- candidate maturity and applicable no-advice language;
- domain and cross-role review with no unresolved critical or major finding;
- an immutable admission manifest and exact assurance binding;
- reference, relation, query, catalog, site, link, and PDF validation;
- preserved one-hot, feature-structure, packed-feature, and learned factored
  controls wherever founding research claims are implicated.

## Batch continuation rule

The program itself is committed. At each batch boundary, review selects among
four execution dispositions for individual candidates:

- **admit** when the owner and source contract pass;
- **merge** when an existing or sibling owner answers the same question;
- **reframe** when the question is useful but the proposed boundary is wrong;
- **replace** from the authorized gap backlog when the candidate does not earn
  canonical ownership.

No ordinary `merge`, `reframe`, or `replace` disposition pauses the next funded
batch. Only a concrete integrity or safety defect blocks the affected artifact,
and that defect does not silently cancel unrelated roadmap work.

## Program acceptance

The roadmap is complete when:

- all six waves have a recorded disposition for every candidate slot;
- approximately 30-36 Tables have been admitted, merged, reframed, or replaced
  under explicit evidence rather than count pressure;
- the final reference has no duplicate owner, unresolved critical/major review
  finding, broken target, stale hash, or invalid publication artifact;
- the resulting proof preserves candidate maturity and professional-advice
  boundaries;
- reader results, including nulls and failures, are recorded without being
  promoted into broader claims.

The next portfolio decision is then based on the resulting coverage and reader
evidence. It does not assume that the shortest path to approximately 250 Tables
is to continue subdividing these domains.
