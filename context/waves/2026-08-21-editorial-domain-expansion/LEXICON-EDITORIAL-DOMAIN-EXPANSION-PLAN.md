# Lexicon Editorial and Domain Expansion Plan

Status: active

## Reader problem

The 37 newest Tables still expose discovery-era labels and self-descriptions,
making the canonical book read like two editorial generations. The reference
also lacks obvious anchors needed to route common questions in epistemology,
cognition, discourse, civil/economic relations, biology, security, material
culture, education, and Earth systems.

## Bounded change

1. Replace discovery-facing `Admission brief` labels with `Editorial record`
   and normalize self-references such as `this candidate` without changing
   sense IDs, factorizations, distinctions, stopping rules, or sources.
2. Keep one consistent `Status: candidate anchor entry` maturity line.
3. Add ten candidate-maturity canonical Tables:
   - knowledge, belief, justification, doubt, ignorance, and understanding;
   - learning, memory, attention, reasoning, and skill;
   - communication, conversation, discourse, narrative, and dialogue;
   - possession, ownership, property interest, agreement, contract, exchange,
     and transaction;
   - market, competition, production, labor, capital, and finance;
   - organism, cell, gene, development, metabolism, and disease mechanism;
   - authentication, threat, attack, defense, and security control;
   - material, object, artifact, tool, and manufacturing process;
   - education, instruction, curriculum, learning objective, and educational
     assessment;
   - geographic place, environment, weather, climate, Earth system, and
     environmental change.
4. Preserve adjacent owners through explicit routing and stopping boundaries.
5. Record the batch in a new admission manifest and bind exact source hashes.
6. Regenerate reference, assurance, catalogs, website, and book projection.
7. Update roadmap scale language to the actual post-wave inventory and retain
   the approximately 250-Table substantial-volume horizon as a horizon, not a
   completion claim.

## Expected scale

The batch is capped at ten new Tables and 56 new senses: 101 canonical Tables
and 680 senses before generated factor counts are known. No new relation kind
or pointer is authorized by the plan.

## Verification

- all normalized entries retain exact semantic sections and source custody;
- ten new entries satisfy the full Table grammar and source review;
- no duplicate entry, sense, or factor identity;
- all references and internal links resolve;
- generated counts match the declared scale;
- reference, assurance, site, PDF, and role checks pass;
- no unresolved P1/P2 result finding.

## Claim boundary

Coverage means a usable routing anchor exists for the named governing
questions. It does not mean any field is exhaustively represented, one theory
is universally correct, or the book provides legal, medical, security,
financial, educational, or environmental advice.
