# Lexicon Candidate Canonical Merge Plan

Status: completed

## Reader and product decision

Readers should be able to find the 37 completed concept families in the same
canonical book and lookup system as the existing 54 Tables. The smallest
coherent change is one batch admission to 91 canonical Tables, retaining
candidate maturity and explicit provenance rather than maintaining a hidden
parallel draft corpus.

## Admission contract

1. Copy each complete draft from `signals/discover/candidates/` to a stable
   Lexicon path under `tables/entries/`.
2. Change only publication status and repository-relative links; retain each
   orientation, sense table, factorization, distinctions, stopping boundary,
   selection procedure, Reference Delta, failure signs, and sources.
3. Add one reference record per entry with stable filename-derived identity,
   declared domain, candidate maturity, exact senses, and root factors.
4. Regenerate catalogs, assurance identities, search, Reader, book, and PDF.
5. Run a pointer census only over exact structural labels in canonical code
   expressions. Admit a new pointer only when it is reusable, non-taxonomic,
   occurs in at least two owning Tables, and has a bounded orientation cue.
6. Preserve the old noncanonical drafts as historical discovery evidence; do
   not delete or rewrite their original admission records.
7. Compare copied Sources and provenance sections with their historical
   drafts, validate required grammar and links, and stop this bulk-admission
   route after the named 37-entry corpus.

## Verification

- exactly 91 unique canonical entry identities and 624 canonical senses;
- all 37 promoted Markdown files satisfy the canonical entry grammar;
- no duplicate sense or factor identity in the interchange;
- all internal links and catalog owners resolve;
- pointer rows are unique, have occurrences, and remain noncanonical;
- candidate, contract, canonical, site, and PDF gates pass;
- final role review reaches fixed point with no P1/P2 findings.

## Claim boundary

This is an editorial admission decision authorized by the repository owner.
It establishes canonical ownership at candidate maturity, not empirical
validity, exhaustiveness, market demand, reader comprehension, or R5 release
readiness.
