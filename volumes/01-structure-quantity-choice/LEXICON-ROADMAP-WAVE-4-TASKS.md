# Lexicon Roadmap Wave 4 Tasks

Status: author-only structural rehearsal; not reader evidence

## Task W4A — Which health-state concept is intended?

Separate health, disease, disorder, condition, diagnosis, and well-being.

## Task W4B — Which functioning level is intended?

Separate body function/impairment, activity limitation, contextual disability,
and participation without inferring from diagnosis.

## Task W4C — Which intervention purpose is intended?

Separate prevention, intervention, treatment, therapy, rehabilitation, delivery,
and effect without choosing care.

## Task W4D — Which care-delivery relation is intended?

Separate care, service, provider/recipient, pathway, plan, continuity, and continuum.

## Task W4E — Which outcome status is intended?

Separate outcome, assessment, endpoint, response, effect, recovery, remission,
cure, and quality of life.

## Rehearsal boundary

Pass establishes routing only and supplies no individual health determination.
