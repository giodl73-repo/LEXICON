# Lexicon Domain Expansion Tasks

Status: author-only structural rehearsal for `sim-69`; not reader evidence

## Task DA — What does the person know or learn?

Separate belief, knowledge, justification, doubt, ignorance, understanding,
learning, memory, attention, reasoning, skill, education, instruction,
curriculum, objective, and assessment without diagnosing a learner.

## Task DB — What communicative organization is present?

Separate communication, conversation, discourse, narrative, and dialogue from
speech-act force, genre, register, interaction protocol, and information flow.

## Task DC — What institutional-economic relation applies?

Separate possession, ownership, interests, agreement, contract, exchange,
transaction, market, competition, production, labor, capital, and finance
under explicit account, authority, framework, and jurisdiction.

## Task DD — What biological or material entity/process is intended?

Separate organism, cell, gene, development, metabolism, disease mechanism,
material, object, artifact, tool, and manufacturing from their adjacent
chemical, system, lifecycle, clinical, and production owners.

## Task DE — What security or Earth-system state is intended?

Separate authentication, threat, attack, defense, control, place, environment,
weather, climate, Earth system, and environmental change while retaining
framework, boundary, scale, reference period, evidence, and uncertainty.

## Rehearsal boundary

These prompts establish structural coverage only. They provide no reader,
professional, empirical, market, or release evidence.
