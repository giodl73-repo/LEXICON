# Lexicon Candidate-Maturity Admission Tasks

Status: author-only structural rehearsal for `sim-68`; not reader evidence

## Task CA — What kind of thing persists or is realized?

Distinguish disposition and function; process aspect; persistence; collection
identity; abstract work and compliant instance; and type, token, realization,
and embodiment without treating one ontology as universal.

## Task CB — What was perceived, felt, intended, or presented?

Separate perception and appearance, appraisal and felt episode, motive and
intention, persona and self-presentation, and human action error and recovery
without diagnosing a person or assigning blame.

## Task CC — What does this expression do and how is it formed?

Separate speech-act force, metaphor and related figures, stance and attributed
affect, register and variety, morpheme and word formation, and recurring
pattern without flattening language, interpretation, and mechanism.

## Task CD — What rule-governed position or institution applies?

Separate consent and withdrawal, deontic status, Hohfeldian incidents,
institutional rules and status functions, privacy and disclosure, trust and
legitimacy, and inheritance or succession under named authority and scope.

## Task CE — What strategic or historical condition shaped the outcome?

Separate incentive and externality, collective provision and appropriation,
path dependence, tipping and propagation, descent and genealogy, and
adaptation and selection without turning a structural account into policy or
causal proof.

## Task CF — What scientific or operational condition is being reported?

Separate ecosystem and niche, clinical observation and diagnosis,
fault/error/failure/incident/outage, and prediction or forecast while retaining
domain authority, evidence, horizon, and uncertainty.

## Task CG — What mathematical or comparative structure is intended?

Separate similarity and equivalence, cardinality and rank, distribution shape
and extremes, and alignment or correspondence without inferring identity or
importance from a score or ordering.

## Task CH — What may be safely routed rather than newly owned?

For every admitted Table, exercise its stopping boundary, failure signs, and
cross-references. Confirm that examples, named taxonomies, mechanisms, and
related concepts remain with their declared owners.

## Rehearsal boundary

These prompts establish structural coverage only. They do not establish
reader findability, comprehension, usefulness, correctness in a live domain,
or release readiness.
