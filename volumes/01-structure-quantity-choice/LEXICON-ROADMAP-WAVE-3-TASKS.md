Separate statement, proposition, premise, conclusion, argument, claim, and evidence.

## Task W3B — What kind of inference is claimed?

Separate deduction, induction, abduction, and analogy, including warrant and defeasibility.

## Task W3C — What formal establishment status applies?

Separate validity, both soundness senses, proof, demonstration, refutation, and failed proof search.

## Task W3D — What compatibility or coverage property applies?

Separate consistency, contradiction, satisfiability, semantic completeness, theory completeness, and unknown.

## Task W3E — Which mathematical level is intended?

Separate number/numeral/quantity/magnitude/measurement and set/collection/membership/relation/function/structure, then redirect operational questions.
