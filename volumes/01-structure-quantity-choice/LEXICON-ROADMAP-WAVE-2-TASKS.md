# Lexicon Roadmap Wave 2 Tasks

Status: author-only structural rehearsal; not reader evidence

## Task W2A — Which political institution is meant?

Separate state, government, polity, administration, and public authority.

## Task W2B — Which person-polity status is meant?

Separate citizenship, nationality, residency, political membership, and
political status under a stated jurisdiction and time.

## Task W2C — What representation relation is claimed?

Separate representative, constituency, mandate, delegation, and election,
including omitted voices and competing standards.

## Task W2D — What kind of political force or standing applies?

Separate power, authority, descriptive or normative legitimacy, influence,
and coercion without inferring obligation.

## Task W2E — Which legal or public-action stage applies?

Separate law, rule, precedent, judgment, remedy, enforcement, policy, program,
service, regulation, implementation, output, and outcome.

## Rehearsal boundary

Pass establishes structural coverage only and gives no legal or political advice.
