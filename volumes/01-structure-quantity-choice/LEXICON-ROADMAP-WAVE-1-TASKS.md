# Lexicon Roadmap Wave 1 Tasks

Status: author-only structural rehearsal; not reader evidence

## Task W1A — Who or what is the social bearer?

Separate an individual, person, actor, group, community, and society while
preserving the entity account, membership basis, recognition authority, and
group-to-member non-transfer rule.

## Task W1B — What maintains the standard or practice?

Separate a social norm, convention, custom, practice, and tradition without
inferring normative force from frequency or treating transmission as copying.

## Task W1C — What kind of evaluation is being made?

Separate good, benefit, welfare, prudential harm, flourishing, equality,
equity, fairness, justice, and desert under explicit subjects, baselines,
currencies, criteria, principles, and competing accounts.

## Task W1D — What attribution or response is at issue?

Separate causal contribution, moral responsibility, answerability, blame,
liability, excuse, justification, and exemption without adjudicating a case.

## Task W1E — What relational process is present?

Separate cooperation, solidarity, reciprocity, conflict, and reconciliation,
including power asymmetry and unresolved justice claims.

## Rehearsal boundary

Passing these tasks establishes structural coverage only. External reader and
domain outcomes remain pending.
